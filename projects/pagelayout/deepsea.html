What are Sea Angels Anyway?

Sea angels (or Clade Gymnosomata) are small free-swimming sea slugs that live about 200 meters under the sea. They get their name from the two wing-like appeandages, making them slightly resemble angels. They reach up to 5 centimeters at most and can be found in warmer waters and migrate from the Sea of Okhotsk to the coast of north Hokkaido in the winter.

Sea angels are carnivorous animals who exclusively hunt thecosomatas, also known as Sea butterflies. Their hunting stratergies can vary, from being ambush predators to actively persuing their prey. Using their "wings" to propel them forward, they can reach speeds up to 100 mm/s.
