Dolly Parton

Interest Hero

Dolly Rebecca Parton Dean, or more simply Dolly Parton, was a major country music singer-songwriter as well as an actress, businesswoman, philanthropist, and all around belevoed icon. She was well known for being a kind and generous soul who always saw the best in everybody she met. She was also a fashion icon, who was beautiful inside and out. She opened up Dollywood in Pigeon Forge, Tennessee to open up more jobs in the area and started the Imagination Library to give free books for children. She was a huge advocate for education and lieracy. Of course, she is also well known for her hit songs "I Will Always Love You", "Jolene", "Coat of Many Colors", and "9 to 5".
