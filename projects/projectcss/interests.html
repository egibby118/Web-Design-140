The Joys of Painting

Interest Hero

Painting is a hobby I have become more and more serious about over the years. Originally I wasn't really interested in it, as I prefered digital art over physical art. But at around junior or senior year of highschool, I had started paying more attention to it thanks to some projects my art teacher assigned to me. I found myself particularly drawn to gouache, a water activated paint that is very pigmented and vibrant. I always describe it as a combination between acrylic and watercolor paint, and I think it is very easy and fun to work with. Some of my favorite pieces are made primarily with gouache.

The subjects that I like to paint can vary. Usually I find myself painting animals and nature. Mostly I have ended up painting my family pets. I like painting our cats, but most of them have tabby patterned fur, making it a bit of a challenge to capture it properly. Fur in general can be a challenge to paint, especially if it's long. But the challenge is another way of making the process more fun and interesting.
