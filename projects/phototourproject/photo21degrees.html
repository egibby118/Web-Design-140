Photo Tour of Oahu

21 Degrees Estate is a cacao farm that sources their cacao beans out to different local chocolate makers. They also do farm tours where you can learn about cacao and chocolate making, and even taste test some local chocolate! They have some goat mascots that hang around during the tour that you can pet and feed. They even wear fun little leis!
