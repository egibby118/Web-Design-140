Photo Tour of Oahu

Me and my family went to the Honolulu Zoo right before Hurricane Lala hit. It was windy and rainy, but the zoo was still very fun and interesting to tour through. We got to meet many beautiful animals before we had to leave. Hopefully next time we visit it won't be with such bad weather!
