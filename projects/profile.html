One of my major interests is creating designs and illustrations. I enjoy drawing digital art on my iPad using the art program Procreate; I usually like to draw character but I also do other types of designs. I also really like painting, usually with gouache which is like a combination of acrylic & watercolor paint. Unfortunately the paint itself can run quite a high price, and the brand I like can add up to $50 for a set of 12!

Another one of my hobbies is gaming, most often on my PC or laptop. One game I have been playing recently is Slime Rancher and its sequal, I think the farm management aspect is really fun and rewarding. One of my all time favorite games is Minecraft, which might be a bit basic of me but it's a really fun and customizable experience that I love to come back to all the time.
