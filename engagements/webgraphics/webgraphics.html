Web Graphics

What kind of photos are good to encode using JPEG?

Nearly all online images and some types of printed photos or artwork. Best for photos taken by cameras.

What kind of photos are good to encode using GIF?

Small website or email graphics or animated/ transparent graphics.

What is unique about the PNG format?

PNGs can support millions more colors than JPEGs can as well as transparency. Sometimes animated elements too but that one is more rare. Best for colorful designs or designs made digitally.

How do you resize your photo to the size it should be displayed?

You should resize it using a differnt editing program.

Why should you resize photos using photo editing software instead of resizing it using CSS?

Because it makes the file unnecessarily bigger and can cause lag, making the picture load slowly and possibly slow down the user's experience.
