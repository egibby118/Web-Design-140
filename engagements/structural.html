    What goes in the Header?

  • Header tags are usually for your company or brand name that you want people to see and remember.

    • What goes in the Nav? Can the Nav go in the header? Does it have to go in the header? Does the Nav contain links to another website?

  • The links to other parts of your site go into the nav tags. Nav tags can go into the header, but they don't have to be they can also be below or above it. Nav can contain links to other websites occasionally.

    • What goes in Main?

  • The main tag is where you put your unique content. It's the main content of your website.

    • What goes in the Footer?

  • Legal things (copyright, policies, disclaimers, etc.), search tags for Google, links to other related websites, links to personal contact info, and similar things are what goes into the Footer.
