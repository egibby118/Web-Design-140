What is the difference between block tags and inline tags?

Block tags start on a new line, add a margin before and after the tag, and takes up as much space as possible. They can have the properties of height, width, boarder, margin and padding. Inline tags don't start on a new line and only takes up as much space as necessary.

What is the margin, border, and padding?

Margin controls how much space is between block tags. Padding controls how far the text is away from the edges of the box. Border controls the look of the box surrounding the box.

What are units of measurement you can use in CSS?

Usually px, but you can also use inches, centimeters, and percents.

When would you use a figure tag?
