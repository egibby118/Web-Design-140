What is normal flow in HTML?

The normal flow is from left to right.

What does float do? Why do you need to clear the float?

Float can move an image and makes the text flow around it. You need to clear so that certain elements don't flow around the image and behaves normally, like the footer.

What do absolute, fixed, and relative do?

Absolute moves the image into a certain spot and over whatever text or other elements are already there. Fixed stays on your screen in that exact position no matter how you move. Relative is similar to float.

How can you make nice navigation buttons for mobile devices?

You can make them fixed so that no matter where they go or how they scroll the buttons will be in the exact same place.
